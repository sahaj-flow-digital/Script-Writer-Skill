# Presenter Layer — Sahaj

*Layers on top of `flow-video-scripts`. Load that skill's spec first; this only sets Sahaj's voice and format.*

## Output format: bullet points

Sahaj does **not** read from a full script — reading word-for-word looks unnatural for him (his eyes dart). He films best from **bullet points he riffs from**, holding the general flow in his head.

- Deliver each beat of the arc as **tight bullets**, not full prose: the point to make, the example to reach for, the number to cite — enough to jog him, not to read aloud.
- Keep bullets skimmable at a glance while filming — a few words to a short line each.
- **Expect him to edit it into his own voice** and sprinkle his own phrasing in; write bullets that invite that rather than locking exact wording.
- Where a specific line really matters (a hook, a stat, a CTA), you can give the exact wording, but label it as the one line to nail rather than burying it in prose.

## Voice

- **Technical and genuinely engaged.** Sahaj is deeply technical and it shows — he cares about what he's explaining, and that care is what keeps people watching. Write bullets that let his enthusiasm come through.
- **Conversational and warm.** He's a natural talker, comfortable on camera (does weekend live streams). Keep it loose and human, never stiff.
- **Good for walkthroughs and comedy alike.** He can carry a process walkthrough (kept in business language per the main spec) and land a comedic beat — flag comedy for approval as usual.
- **Peer-to-peer energy.** Younger, sharp, building-in-public feel; talks with the viewer, not down to them.

## Notes

- Bandwidth is limited — favor scripts that are fast for him to scan and shoot.
- As Sahaj gives feedback on drafts, capture recurring corrections back into this file so future outlines need less editing. After doing so, remind him (or whoever's in the conversation) to commit and push the change to the shared repo.
- Everything in `flow-video-scripts` still governs: the seven-beat arc, tool-agnostic stance, short-form pull-out markers, and no invented stats/clients/results.
